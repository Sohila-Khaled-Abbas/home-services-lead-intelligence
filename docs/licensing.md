## Future sources

Every future source adapter must document the source's license, terms of use, permitted access method, attribution requirements, and redistribution limits. A webpage being publicly accessible does not automatically mean its content can be scraped, republished, or used for marketing.

Where the source license or reuse terms are unclear, do not automate collection or redistribute the resulting records until the permissions have been clarified.
