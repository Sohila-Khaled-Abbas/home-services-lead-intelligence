# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Give actionable feedback and explain technical disagreements professionally.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal or sensitive information.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discrimination, intimidation, unwanted personal attention, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

The project maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior that violates this code.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact options on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Sohila-Khaled-Abbas). Do not post private or sensitive information in a public issue.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related interactions.
